Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pro Case Converter ("we," "our," or "us") handles information when you use https://procaseconverter.online/.

1. How The Website Works

Pro Case Converter is primarily a browser-based text tool. The text you type or paste into the workspace is processed in your browser for case conversion, formatting, counting, copying, and downloading features.

2. Text You Enter

We do not require account registration to use the site. Your text is not submitted through a login system or stored in a user profile. Most text operations happen locally in your browser.

3. Local Browser Storage

If you enable the Continue where I left off option, the website stores your editor content and certain workspace preferences in your browser's local storage on your device. This may include your text, undo and redo history, theme choice, font size, text alignment, and formatting preferences. This local storage is used to restore your workspace when you return to the site.

If you disable that option, the website removes the stored autosave items used for that feature. Theme and some interface preferences may still remain in local storage to preserve your selected experience.

4. Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand general site usage, such as page visits, browser information, and interaction patterns. This helps us improve the website, its content, and its tools. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies according to Google's own policies.

5. Cookies And Similar Technologies

The site may use cookies, analytics tags, and browser storage to support measurement, performance, and user preference features. You can control cookies and browser storage through your browser settings.

6. Third-Party Services

When third-party services such as Google Analytics load on the site, those providers may process technical information according to their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of third-party providers you interact with through the website.

7. Data Security

We aim to keep the site straightforward and minimize data collection. However, no method of internet transmission or device storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

The website is a general-purpose text utility and is not intentionally directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through a registration system.

9. Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect website changes, feature updates, or legal and operational needs. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us at support@procaseconverter.online.

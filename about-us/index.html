About Us

Pro Case Converter is a free browser-based text conversion and editing workspace built for people who need to clean, format, count, and export text quickly. The site is designed for writers, students, developers, marketers, SEO users, office teams, and anyone working with copied or drafted text.

What The Website Does

Pro Case Converter lets you paste or type text and instantly transform it using a wide set of case tools, including Sentence case, lower case, UPPER CASE, Capitalized Case, Title Case, Snake_Case, Kebab-Case, Train-Case, dot.case, path/case, space case, flatcase, UPPERFLATCASE, StudlyCaps Random Case, Camel Case, Pascal Case, SCREAMING_SNAKE_CASE, inverse case, alternating case, Reverse Case, Remove Emoji, and Remove HTML.

Workspace Features

  • Fast text conversion: Change text format with a single click and switch back using the same tool when available.
  • Live counters: Track characters, characters without spaces, words, lines, paragraphs, and sentences while editing.
  • Editor controls: Use bold, italic, alignment controls, undo, redo, and adjustable font size inside the workspace.
  • Browser-based convenience: Copy the result to your clipboard, download it as a plain text file, or clear the workspace when you are done.
  • Optional local autosave: If you enable "Continue where I left off," the site stores your progress in your own browser so you can return later.
  • Theme support: Switch between dark mode and light mode for a more comfortable writing experience.

How We Built It

The website is built to do its main work directly in the browser. That means your text conversions, editor actions, and most of your workflow happen on your device instead of being submitted through a user account system or a text processing backend.

Who It Is For

This website is useful for everyday writing cleanup, programming-related naming formats, SEO copy preparation, note formatting, social post editing, and document preparation. Whether you are fixing capitalization, converting variable names, removing emoji, stripping HTML, or checking text length, the goal is to keep the process simple and quick.

Our Goal

Our goal is to offer a practical text utility that is easy to use, works well on desktop and mobile, and helps users finish text editing tasks without installing extra software.
